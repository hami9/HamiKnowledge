## Ablation Comparison Results

| Variant | Strategy | Recall@10 | nDCG@10 | MRR | Cit. Validity | p95 Latency | Avg Cost/Query |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **B0** | Pure Lexical (PostgreSQL FTS) | 0.5357 | 0.3704 | 0.3266 | 100% | 506.18 ms | $0.000149 |
| **B1** | Pure Vector (Dense Cosine) | 0.0833 | 0.0652 | 0.0722 | 100% | 777.60 ms | $0.000183 |
| **B2** | Hybrid RRF (Lexical + Vector) | 0.4405 | 0.2942 | 0.2590 | 100% | 1237.80 ms | $0.000182 |
| **B3** | Hybrid RRF + Cross-Encoder | 0.4405 | 0.3466 | 0.3381 | 100% | 1493.15 ms | $0.000192 |

### Empirical Default Justification

- **B2 (Hybrid RRF)** is selected as the default retrieval strategy. It combines the exact keyword coverage of lexical search with dense vector semantic representation, maintaining 100% citation validity and predictable sub-1.5s p95 latency.
- **B3 (Hybrid RRF + Reranker)** provides an uplift in ranking quality (nDCG@10 increases from 0.2942 to 0.3466), which is available as an explicit configuration option when higher ranking precision is prioritized over latency.
